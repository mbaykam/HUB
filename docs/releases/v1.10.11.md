# HUB 1.10.11

Video calls. During a voice call the iPhone can share its live camera. The
phone recognises text, panels, objects and the user's fingertip on its own,
numbers them on each frame and sends about one marked frame a second, kept
briefly in memory. The assistant reads just the numbered list as text when
that is enough, looks at a small overview otherwise, and can zoom into one
grid cell or mark for detail; zooms are cropped on this PC from the full
frame. With hub_show_overlay it pins arrows, rings, labels and numbered steps
to real objects in the user's camera view; the phone keeps them in place as
it moves.

HUB can reach you properly now. iPhone notifications show what happened and what is needed, grouped per chat, with buttons to reply, approve or deny, or snooze. Assistants can notify you with hub_notify, the agenda sends reminders before events and a morning brief, and you choose which kinds reach you and set quiet hours.

The Apps page at /apps keeps the web apps HUB builds for you; any of them can be shared as an expiring here.now link. Archived chats can be restored. Claude Code runs HUB characters on your Claude subscription with HUB's own tools. Every assistant follows the same craft standards for code, writing and design. In voice calls, finished background tasks are announced even while HUB waits for its wake word, and you can answer without saying it.
