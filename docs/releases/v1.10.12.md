# HUB 1.10.12

Desktop screenshots work again when the PC has been left alone. After ten idle minutes Windows starts its screen saver on a separate desktop, and while it runs no screen capture is possible. HUB now closes it first, just as moving the mouse would, and then captures the real screen. In voice calls the assistant takes the screenshot itself instead of handing it to a helper that was not allowed to.

Apps open on the iPhone through a tunnel. HUB tells proxies such as Cloudflare not to rewrite app pages, and the iPhone app loads them in a form proxies leave alone.

The Usage page shows your Claude subscription's 5-hour and weekly limits, as Claude Code last reported them during one of HUB's Claude Code replies.
