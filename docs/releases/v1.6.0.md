# HUB 1.6.0

The home screen now combines a Knowledge overview with a personal agenda and
to-do list. Written chats open the conversation without dashboard cards. Voice
keeps your planner and shows the assistant's observable work steps separately.

- Browse the complete live Unlid catalogue. Add a model for a character without
  replacing its current selection. Prices, reasoning controls, paused models and
  chat-only endpoints are distinguished. Saved catalogues survive refresh failures.
- Manage near-term and longer-term to-dos and dated calendar events from Home or
  the left sidebar. Completion, edits and recoverable removal persist across restarts.
  Conflicting edits preserve the newer record; failed saves keep the draft.
- Agents can manage requested personal commitments with `hub_planner` and publish
  per-conversation steps with `hub_work_plan`. Native `todo_write` steps also appear
  in this area. Existing character tool permissions still apply.
- Chat uploads reach Knowledge AI with their original-file references. Supported
  document excerpts can produce attributed, verbatim notes with their source chat,
  time, reason and exact quotation. Long document fragments cannot masquerade as
  personal declarations. Original chats and files remain unchanged.
- Excel previews resolve shared strings and cell values without executing formulas.
  Missing, modified, corrupt and unsupported files cannot supply invented evidence.
- Foreground voice admits new speech while retaining playback-text echo checks;
  background mode continues to close Soniox and use the local wake listener.
  Soniox endpoint settings allow more time for utterance endings.

Calendar entries are organizational records, not execution schedules or guaranteed
notifications. Use Tasks for scheduled or recurring agent work. Knowledge stores
original uploads up to 25 MB; local text/document previews have bounded coverage.
Images need a vision-capable character, and audio/video need explicit supported
media inspection. Retaining a file does not imply that every format was understood.

Voice protocol and audio simulations are distinct from physical phone acceptance.
Speakerphone echo cancellation varies by device; test interruption and mixed-language
utterance endings on your phone before relying on them throughout the day.
