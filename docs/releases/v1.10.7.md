# HUB 1.10.7

Your iPhone becomes your assistants' sense organ. With Phone context on in
the HUB app, an assistant can ask for where you are, the place name, nearby
places, battery and network; HUB keeps only the latest reading and says how
old it is. While you share your screen in a voice call, an assistant can mark
areas on it, and the iPhone shows the marked screenshot as a notification
over the app you are in.
