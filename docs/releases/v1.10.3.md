# HUB 1.10.3

The phone composer keeps voice, Send and Stop in their own positions. Tapping
Stop leaves the keyboard closed, and returning Home resets partially opened
drawers. When iPhone loading fails or stalls on a blank page, HUB offers a retry.

Browser cancellation ends a turn even when the browser stops acknowledging
requests. If browser cleanup cannot be confirmed, HUB reports the uncertain
outcome and blocks further browser actions in that conversation until the browser
process reconnects. The Cloudflare gateway closes broken WebSocket connections
without letting their socket errors interrupt the desktop host.

HUB can show a requested workspace screenshot directly in its conversation.
A conversation usage request reports saved model tokens and confirmed OpenRouter
charges. Missing receipts are labeled, and looking them up starts no inference.
