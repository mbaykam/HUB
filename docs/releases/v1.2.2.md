# HUB 1.2.2

Fix spoken delivery of background results. A HUB worker can send its findings to
Mira before its completion notice arrives. Voice now recognizes that native
relay and keeps Mira's answer if another notice arrives in the same turn.
Previously only the later closing remark could reach speech playback.

Results still wait for the conversation to be available, keep their existing
cursor so they are not replayed, and do not start another task. Ordinary spoken
answers and background results retain separate delivery paths. Saved models,
voices, character instructions and conversations are unchanged by this patch.
