# HUB 1.10.15

When HUB needs your decision, it asks in the chat and waits for your next message, on any device. Before, its question appeared only on the PC's HUB window, so a chat on your phone could hang until someone answered on the PC.

Scheduling is more reliable: the automation tool now tells the assistant exactly what it got wrong, so a request like "check this in three days" no longer takes a dozen failed attempts.
