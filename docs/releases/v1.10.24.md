# HUB 1.10.24

Scheduling becomes simple: reminders, scheduled jobs and a quiet heartbeat replace goals, schedules and conditions. Every chat gets one identity and one tool per job, Camofox becomes the main web search, research reads in parallel with fallbacks for blocked pages, your profile updates itself (undoably), and recall uses your latest message. Pairs with iPhone build 24 (smooth Obsidian-style graph, messages that stay visible while HUB is busy, speaker audio in calls, a three-row Models screen and the new Schedule screen).
