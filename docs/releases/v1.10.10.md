# HUB 1.10.10

Knowledge AI now writes a librarian's note for each source: what it was
about, what was kept and under which subject and why, and what was left out.
A daily domain review tidies the domains on its own: it renames unclear ones,
nests narrow ones, merges duplicates and retires empty ones, explains the
change in Knowledge Activity, and every change can be undone. A desktop
screenshot request in the user's own words, like "Masaüstünün bir ekran
görüntüsünü al", is understood, and a refused capture no longer loops.
