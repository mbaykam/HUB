# HUB 1.10.33

## Assistant name
The assistant (the main character) can be renamed from the phone: Settings → Assistant. The name is used in every mode's persona, and the Telegram identity no longer forces "HUB" as the assistant's name. A hidden character that already used the name gives way (for example "Vera (voice)"). New installs still start with "HUB".

## Briefing
`/api/hub/briefing` (header `x-hub-briefing: 1`) keeps the topics in `~/.dsh/hub-briefing/briefing.json` and fetches up to four headlines per topic from Google News RSS (Turkish edition, last two days) when the phone looks and the headlines are older than three hours. A feed that fails keeps its last headlines. No model is called. The assistant gets `hub_briefing_topics` (list, add, remove); topics it adds are marked.

## Video
`hub_media_inspect` without a time range now gives an overview of the whole video (up to 3 hours): captions in the original language when the platform has them (otherwise local Whisper base over up to an hour of sound), and 12 (24 for videos over ten minutes) moments chosen where the picture changes most, on one or two labelled contact sheets. The transcript is shortened evenly to about 14,000 characters. A time range still gives the old close look (six frames, 120 seconds). Links from YouTube (also Shorts), Instagram, TikTok, X and Vimeo are accepted; attachments may be MP4, MOV or WebM. The iPhone compresses picked videos before sending.
