# HUB 1.6.3

The voice adapter previously submitted every Soniox endpoint immediately, even
when local voice activity still indicated that the caller was speaking. A short
continuation could become a separate turn and cause an answer to start too soon.

Soniox now uses lower endpoint sensitivity. HUB holds a confirmed endpoint for
350 ms and, while local speech remains active, waits for the caller to stop.
If another segment arrives before submission, its confirmed words join the same
utterance; interim words remain provisional until Soniox finalizes them. Local
voice activity can delay submission, but cannot finalize an uncertain transcript.

Background, mute, hangup and connection failure discard pending submissions.
Existing local wake listening, foreground interruption, echo protection,
multilingual STT and saved character/model/voice choices remain in place.

Regression checks reproduce a premature endpoint, a delayed final word and the
real Pipecat controller/aggregator path with simulated acoustic activity. These
are protocol simulations, not physical microphone acceptance. A phone test is
still required to assess recognition quality and the added settling delay.

Endpoint tuning follows Soniox's [endpoint detection documentation](https://soniox.com/docs/stt/rt/endpoint-detection).
