# HUB 1.9.1

Inline conversation visuals keep their full height instead of collapsing into a
thin outline. Interactive content, expansion and the original conversation stay
available together.
The Canvas button brings the visual into view without resetting its inputs;
returning from full screen also reveals the inline card.

Automatic chat titles now wait until the reply has finished and the conversation
pauses for 20 seconds. A completed visual supplies its subject directly;
otherwise a small request to the conversation's current model summarizes its
topic. Model requests are limited to one every two minutes per active chat;
follow-up messages cancel outdated work. Provider failures keep the current
title. Manually named chats are preserved.

When reopening an older automatically named chat, a completed visual can repair
its greeting title without a model request. The archive is not bulk-renamed.
