# HUB 1.3.1

Spoken replies use the native 48 kHz playback rate for PWA and Telegram calls.
Local buffered synthesis converts a complete utterance directly to that rate,
avoiding the extra streaming conversion that could retain and discard a word's
ending during a gap before the next clause. Speech recognition remains at16 kHz.

An unavailable sentence tokenizer no longer interrupts a spoken text chunk.
HUB preserves the buffered characters and continues with bounded word cuts,
without disabling the tokenizer's filesystem protection or changing user data.

The selected model, voice, language policy and synthesis quality stay unchanged.
Checks cover missing/denied tokenizer data, streamed numbers and URLs, complete
audio tails, raw WebRTC/Telegram frame conservation, interruption and wake mode.
Cached English/Turkish audio through the real TTS queues is a simulation; actual
phone audio and network behavior still need acceptance.
