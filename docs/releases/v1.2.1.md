# HUB 1.2.1

This patch repairs voice-to-HUB photo delegation and improves the bilingual
conversation and phone camera workflows introduced in 1.2.0.

- Authorized voice tasks can reach their native HUB worker without being blocked
  by the character's outer tool allow-list. Other permissions stay in force.
- Settings → Speech offers **English + Türkçe only** recognition for short and
  long utterances. Automatic spoken replies receive each final utterance's
  language without changing the user's saved language preferences.
- Mira's built-in spoken guidance avoids repeated introductions, language-switch
  announcements and emoji.
- The camera uses a larger preview, preserves the whole frame, and lets you
  review or retake a photo before **Send to HUB**. Retrying keeps the same request
  and does not duplicate work. Voice audio remains independently connected.

See [speech settings](../VOICE_LANGUAGES.md) and
[photos, files and camera](../UPLOADS_AND_CAMERA.md) for operation and recovery.
Recognition can still mishear words. Synthetic audio and browser camera tests
do not establish physical phone microphone, speaker-echo or camera quality.
