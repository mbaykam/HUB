# HUB 1.8.0

- Choose the active character directly beside the attachment button, or from
  voice controls. A running reply retains its admitted identity; the selection
  applies to the following reply. Conversation context and drafts stay in place.
- Simplify the composer by removing its access-level selector. Existing native
  permissions and approval checks still apply.
- Start bounded, background research from **+ → Research…**. Choose depth,
  researcher, sources and freshness. Auto follows configured delegation, so the
  voice assistant can keep talking while its main assistant researches.
- Return to **+ → Research tasks** for progress, pause/resume/stop, source
  passages and a downloadable report. Saved evidence and native work sessions
  survive reconnects. Citation checks require retained read passages and do not
  claim to prove factual truth. See [Research](../research.md).

The research sheet currently accepts text questions. Physical microphone and
phone-speaker performance require device acceptance; simulated voice routing
checks do not establish audio quality.
