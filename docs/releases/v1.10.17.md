# HUB 1.10.17

HUB now learns about your life from ordinary conversation: the people you mention and what is going on with them, and your routines, activities and plans. Before, only preferences, decisions, projects and learning were kept, so a friend's news or a weekly lesson mentioned in passing was forgotten.

Learning no longer stalls. A chat waiting on you, or one slow model request, used to pause all background learning and automations until HUB restarted. Includes 1.10.15 and 1.10.16.
